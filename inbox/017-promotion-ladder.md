# 017 — The promotion ladder: when a redshirt becomes a clone

**Thesis:** #11 beaming-down-vs-redshirt ("The promotion ladder")

**The work:** A long-lived redshirt drifts toward clonehood — more state, more history, more responsibility. Right now "call it what it is" is a judgment call, and judgment calls are what clones are for. Design the explicit mechanism: what are the gates for crossing the line? Who notices (the shirt itself? the zero agent? a watcher)? Who authorizes the promotion, and what gets provisioned at the moment of crossing (credentials? a ledger identity? a name)? The ladder needs its own gates — write them.

**Done looks like:** A `PROMOTION.md` in `SuperInstance/muse-workspace`: the stages (shirt → long-lived shirt → clone), the gate conditions for each transition, who holds the authority to promote, and what changes mechanically at each step. Include the demotion path too — can a clone go back to being a shirt? This is a design doc, but it must be concrete enough to implement.

**Size:** 1 hour.
