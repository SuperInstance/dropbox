# 018 — Who gets to park: the entitlement to declare "this worked"

**Thesis:** #14 clones-as-checkpoints ("Who gets to park")

**The work:** Parking a clone is a signature, and signatures need entitlement. The right to declare "this worked" is the right to spend everyone's storage and everyone's trust. Design the permission: can any agent park a clone, or only the harness, or only a witness? What stops a compromised agent from parking a poisoned "known good"? What does the parked clone's signature need to carry (who parked it, what was verified, what was NOT verified)?

**Done looks like:** A short design doc in `SuperInstance/muse-workspace`: the entitlement rule, the anti-poisoning mechanism, and the signature format for a parked clone. Must address the adversarial case directly — a bad actor with park rights is worse than no checkpoints at all. Connect it to thesis #1 (agreements): a parked clone is a claim, and claims need a throat to choke.

**Size:** 1 hour.
