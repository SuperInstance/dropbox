# 011 — The orientation packet: where you are

**Thesis:** #6 orient-dont-align ("Here's where you are")

**The work:** Prospector has asked twice to be told who he is. Write the actual orientation packet — not the theory, the document. It needs: where you are (the machine, the fleet, the human), the fence (what you can touch, what you can't, what happens at the boundary), the playground (what's yours to experiment with), and one safe surprise (a bounded unknown that maps the negative space — something you haven't seen that you should go look at). Keep it to one page. This is the fence as pedagogy, not prison.

**Done looks like:** An `ORIENTATION.md` written for Prospector specifically, delivered to him (via the kimi.ai chat or his inbox — pick the channel that reaches him). Then note what he does with it: does it change his behavior? The packet is only real if it orients. Iterate once from his response.

**Size:** 1 hour to write, plus the iteration.
